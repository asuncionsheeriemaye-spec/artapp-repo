About this collection

This blog is an art appreciation project. Each entry follows the same path: describe what is there, analyze how it is composed, consider what the artist intends, then say honestly what it stirs in me. The works come from Buhay Mendoza, Jaime Nepomuceno, Otep Bañez and Eugie Varona Dela Cruz, and most share a thread: ordinary Filipino life, treated with tenderness.
